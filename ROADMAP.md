# Pocket Arcade roadmap

The first four games are live. These are the next original games to add:

1. **Desktop Lamb** — a tiny old-school desktop-pet game: guide a lamb around
   a cozy field, feed it, and keep it happy. This is a new character and world,
   inspired by the desktop-pet format rather than a port of eSheep or another
   commercial game.
2. **Pixel Pinball** — one-screen pinball with combo lanes and a local high
   score.
3. **Moon Miner** — tap rocks, chain gems, and upgrade your tiny rover.
4. **Garden Grid** — a relaxing timed pattern puzzle.
5. **Rhythm Tiles** — tap the glowing tiles in time with an original beat.
6. **Orbital Golf** — sling a ball around miniature planets into the target.
7. **Tiny Kart** — a short top-down time-trial circuit with three original
   tracks.

Every new game will use original names, art, sound, and copy. Open-source
engines may be used under their licenses, but commercial ROMs, ripped assets,
trademarked characters, and unlicensed music are out of scope.
